# CAFE: Advance for Future Issuance Agreement

*Contrato de Adiantamento para Futura Emissão*

An open startup investment agreement template under Brazilian law. The investment is irrevocable, is not debt and converts into equity upon the events defined in the agreement.

CAFE is based on the Brazilian Startup Legal Framework (Complementary Law 182/2021), the Brazilian Civil Code and the Economic Freedom Law (Law 13,874/2019). It works for limited liability companies (LTDA) and corporations (S/A).

## Why CAFE?

The Startup Legal Framework provides for several investment instruments. One of the most widely used, the convertible loan (mútuo conversível), is a debt agreement. That nature creates uncertainty for the parties and has already brought many disputes to the courts.

CAFE is an alternative to it. Inspired by the American SAFE and by the Brazilian AFAC (advance for future capital increase), CAFE is neither debt nor revenue of the startup. The investment is an advance of funds allocated to share capital, which converts into equity upon the events provided for in the agreement.

The rationale behind the template is explained in the article [CAFE: an open startup investment agreement in which the investment is not debt](docs/en/article.md). The original article in Portuguese is on the [author's blog](https://guilhermedeoliveira.com.br/cafe-modelo-aberto-contrato-investimento-startups/).

Before signing, the agreement shows the stake the Investor will receive and the dilution of each shareholder, other investor and vesting beneficiary. The calculator does the math and fills in Schedule V.

The text uses plain language, short paragraphs and visual highlights. The idea is that founders and investors understand what they are signing.

## How to use

1. Read the "CAFE on one page" section and the "Before signing" guide.
2. Fill in the summary table and choose the options in brackets.
3. Use the calculator to build the cap table and the dilution in Schedule V.
4. Have the agreement reviewed by a lawyer and an accountant before signing.

CAFE is a starting point, not a ready-made agreement. Each transaction has its own particularities and calls for adaptation.
